# Definitions

**Connection** — relation between elements that constrains or enables interaction.

**Topology** — organization of connections.

**Structure** — stable or recurring organization of elements and connections.

**Emergence** — system-level pattern produced by local interactions without prescribing the final global pattern.

**Attractor** — recurrent dynamical regime toward which trajectories converge.

**Basin** — initial states reaching an attractor.

These are operational working definitions and may be revised.
