# CONNECTIONS — Relation & Structure Research

**Status:** RESEARCH / ACTIVE

## Purpose

Исследование связей и структур, возникающих из взаимодействия элементов.

EN: Research of connections and structures emerging from element interactions.

中文：研究元素相互作用产生的连接与结构。

## Scope

CONNECTIONS studies relations as first-class research objects:

- connections between distinguishable elements;
- formation and persistence of structures;
- topology and structural change;
- dynamics of relational systems;
- emergence and stability;
- relation between structure and observable behavior.

## Boundary

CONNECTIONS is a research repository.

It is **not**:

- the universal SYSTEM-FOUNDATION;
- the canonical SPACE runtime;
- Guardian;
- a second internal language;
- a source of physical laws without independent validation.

Research results remain local until they pass the evidence and promotion process.

## Evidence discipline

Use:

`FACT → CHECK → RESULT → DECISION → FIXATION`

For experiments, preserve the lineage:

`FAMILY → EXPERIMENT → RUN → RESULT → AUDIT/CONTROL → REPLICATION → STATUS`

Planned or coded work is not an experimental result. Executed work is not automatically validated evidence.

## Relation to other repositories

- **Ω-Lab** — experiments and evidence generation.
- **Ω-Math** — formal mathematical language and typed relational semantics.
- **CICADA-LANGUAGE** — internal graph-native interaction semantics for SPACE.
- **SPACE-** — canonical executable organism; CONNECTIONS does not modify its authority.

- **ORISIK** — preservation, reconciliation and historical source material.

## Current principle

A connection is an observed or constructed relation in a declared model. Its existence in a graph does not by itself establish causality, physical interaction or universal law.

## First work

Start from explicit topology, dynamics, controls, provenance and reproducibility rather than interpretation.

Every promoted result must identify:

`SOURCE → PROTOCOL → RUN → RESULT → VALIDATION → PROMOTION DECISION`
